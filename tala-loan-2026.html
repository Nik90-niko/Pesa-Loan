Tala Loan App Review 2026 - How To Apply & Get Approved

Updated: November 2026 | By PesaLoan254

Tala is one of the most popular loan apps in Kenya. You can borrow from Ksh 1,000 up to Ksh 100,000+ directly from your phone, without collateral, without a guarantor, and get money in minutes to your M-Pesa.

How To Apply For Tala Loan

  1. Download Tala App from Google Play Store
  2. Register with your Safaricom number and ID
  3. Allow Tala to check your M-Pesa messages for credit scoring
  4. Fill in your personal details and monthly income
  5. Apply - You will get a loan offer in 2 minutes
  6. Accept and money is sent to your M-Pesa

Tala Loan Limit 2026

New users start at Ksh 1,000 - Ksh 5,000. If you repay on time, your limit grows fast:

  • 1st Loan: Ksh 1,000 - 5,000
  • After 3 good repayments: Ksh 10,000 - 20,000
  • After 6+ repayments: Ksh 30,000 - 100,000

How To Increase Your Tala Limit

  • Repay on time or early: This is the #1 factor
  • Borrow and repay often: Don't stay for months without a loan
  • Keep M-Pesa active: Use M-Pesa daily for sending, bills, airtime
  • Update your income: If your income increased, update it in the app
  • Don't have other unpaid loans: Clear other apps before applying

Tala Interest & Repayment

Tala charges interest based on your loan amount and days. For example, Ksh 10,000 for 30 days can cost around Ksh 1,500 - Ksh 1,800 in interest. Always check the exact total in the app before you accept.

To repay: Open Tala App > Tap Repay > Pay with M-Pesa. You can also repay early - there is no penalty.

Tala Requirements

  • Kenyan ID - 18 years and above
  • Safaricom line with M-Pesa
  • Android phone
  • Good M-Pesa history

Is Tala Safe & Legit?

Yes. Tala is licensed by the Central Bank of Kenya (CBK) as a Digital Credit Provider. It is safe to use, but you must repay on time to avoid CRB listing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my Tala application rejected?
Low M-Pesa activity, unpaid loans in other apps, or CRB listing. Wait 7 days, clear other loans, and try again.

Can I get Tala if I am on CRB?
No. Clear your CRB first.

How long does Tala take?
2-10 minutes to M-Pesa after approval.

Final Advice

Tala is good for emergencies. Don't borrow to pay another loan. Borrow only what you can repay in 30 days.
