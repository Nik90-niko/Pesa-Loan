How to Apply for Timiza Loan

Option 1 - USSD (No Internet):
1. Dial *848# on your Safaricom line
2. Select "Timiza" > "Loans"
3. Enter amount and confirm
4. Money comes instantly

Option 2 - App:
1. Download Timiza app from Play Store
2. Register with ID & phone number
3. Apply for loan in the app
