Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 17, 2026

At PesaLoan254, we respect your privacy. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you for your ID number, M-Pesa PIN, or bank password. Never share these with anyone.

When you visit our site, we may collect:

  • Your device type and browser
  • Pages you visit on our site
  • Your name and phone number ONLY if you fill our contact form

2. How We Use Your Information

We use it to improve our website, respond to your messages, and show you relevant loan comparisons.

3. Cookies

We use cookies, including Google Analytics and Google AdSense cookies, to understand how visitors use our site and to show ads. You can turn off cookies in your browser settings.

4. Third-Party Links

Our site links to loan apps like Tala, Branch, and M-Shwari. When you click those links, their own privacy policies apply. We are not responsible for their sites.

5. We Are Not A Lender

PesaLoan254 is a comparison website only. We do not offer loans, we do not approve loans, and we do not store your loan application details.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, email us at: info@pesaloan254.co.ke
