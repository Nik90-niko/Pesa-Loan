Understanding the application process, repayments and important considerations.

What is Branch?

Branch is a digital financial services platform that offers products in selected markets. Users should check the official application to confirm which services are currently available in Kenya and whether they qualify.

How to apply

  1. Download the official Branch application from a trusted app store.
  2. Register using the information requested in the app.
  3. Review the available loan offer and eligibility requirements.
  4. Read the total repayment amount and fees.
  5. Accept only if you understand the terms and can repay.

What should you check before borrowing?

  • The total amount you will repay.
  • Interest and any additional fees.
  • The repayment deadline.
  • Late payment consequences.
  • Privacy and permissions requested by the app.
  • Whether the provider is properly authorized to operate.

Are Branch loans guaranteed?

No. Loan eligibility, limits and approval are determined by the provider. Having an account or installing an app does not guarantee that you will receive a loan.

Is Branch safe?

Before sharing personal information, confirm that you are using the official application. Review its privacy policy, permissions, terms of service and applicable regulatory information. Avoid unofficial agents asking you to pay money to guarantee loan approval.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply using my phone?

The provider's official application is the place to check current application requirements and availability.

How much can I borrow?

Loan limits vary by customer and provider assessment. Confirm any available amount inside the official app.

What happens if I repay late?

Late repayment may result in additional charges or other consequences under the lender's terms. Contact the provider if you expect difficulties meeting a repayment date.
