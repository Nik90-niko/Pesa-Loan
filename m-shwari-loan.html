Quick Facts

How To Apply - Step By Step

Step 1: Dial *234# on your Safaricom line
Step 2: Select Loans > Request Loan
Step 3: Enter amount you want (e.g. 5000)
Step 4: Enter M-Pesa PIN to confirm
Step 5: Money comes to M-Pesa in seconds ✅

If you have no limit, first save on M-Shwari for 2-4 weeks, then limit appears.

How To Check M-Shwari Limit

  1. Dial *234#
  2. Select Loans
  3. Select Check Loan Limit
  4. Your limit shows instantly

How To Increase M-Shwari Limit

  • ✅ Save small money on M-Shwari every week
  • ✅ Borrow small and repay ON TIME
  • ✅ Use M-Pesa often - Lipa Na M-Pesa, Send Money
  • ✅ Never default - 1 default kills limit
  • ✅ After 3 good loans, limit goes up

FAQs

Q: Can I get M-Shwari without savings?
Yes, but limit will be very small (100-500). Save first to grow it.
Q: What if I fail to pay?
You get 30 days + 30 days extension. After that, you are listed. Always pay on time.
Q: M-Shwari vs Fuliza which is better?
M-Shwari is cheaper (7.5% once). Fuliza is 1% per day - expensive. Use M-Shwari first.
