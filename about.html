About PesaLoan254

PesaLoan254 helps Kenyans compare the best loan apps in Kenya before they borrow.

We are NOT a lender. We do not give loans. We help you compare Tala, Branch, M-Shwari, Timiza and others so you can see interest, limits and repayment time in one place.

Our Mission

To stop Kenyans from borrowing from expensive, hidden-fee lenders. We show clear, honest comparison.

How We Make Money

When you click Apply and get a loan, the lender may pay us a small commission. This does not change your loan cost.
