Borrowing Tips

Before taking a loan

Always understand the full repayment amount, due date, fees and consequences of late payment.


Borrow only what you can reasonably repay. Avoid taking another loan to repay an existing loan unless you fully understand the costs involved.


Compare multiple providers and read their official terms before making a borrowing decision.
