How to Compare Loan Providers

Before borrowing, compare the total amount you will repay, not only the advertised loan amount.

Review interest, fees, due dates, late-payment consequences, privacy practices and customer support.

Always confirm the lender's current terms through its official channel.

Borrow Money Safely

Never share your PIN, password or one-time verification code. Be cautious of anyone demanding an upfront release fee.

Read app permissions and check that you are using the genuine lender app or website. If an offer seems suspicious, pause and verify.
