FAQs

Q: Can I get Tala without CRB?
Tala checks CRB. If you are listed, clear it first or try M-Shwari / KCB M-Pesa.
Q: What if I fail to pay Tala?
You get late fees and CRB listing. Always pay on time or contact Tala to extend.
Q: Tala vs Branch which is better?
Tala gives first loan faster. Branch gives higher limit later. Use Tala first, then Branch.
Q: Can I have Tala and Branch at same time?
Yes, you can have both. Many people do.
