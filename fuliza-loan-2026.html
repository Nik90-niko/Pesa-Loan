How To Borrow Fuliza Loan 2026 - Increase Your Limit

Updated: November 2026 | By PesaLoan254

Fuliza is Safaricom's overdraft service that lets you complete your M-Pesa transaction even when you don't have enough money. If you are sending money, paying a bill, or buying airtime and your balance is low, Fuliza covers the difference.

How To Borrow Fuliza For The First Time

You do NOT need to download an app. Follow these steps:

  1. Dial *234# on your Safaricom line
  2. Select Fuliza M-Pesa
  3. Select Opt In
  4. Accept Terms and Conditions
  5. You will get an SMS with your Fuliza limit

Once you opt in, Fuliza will work automatically. Next time you try to send money and you don't have enough, Fuliza will complete it for you.

How To Check Your Fuliza Limit

Dial *234# > Fuliza M-Pesa > Check My Limit. You can also check your balance with *234# > My Balance to see your Fuliza balance and your M-Pesa balance.

How To Increase Your Fuliza Limit

Safaricom gives you a limit based on how you use M-Pesa. To increase it:

  • Use M-Pesa daily: Send money, pay bills, buy airtime with M-Pesa
  • Repay on time: Always repay Fuliza quickly, don't wait days
  • Keep your line active: Don't stay for weeks without transacting
  • Save with M-Shwari: Saving and repaying loans on time builds trust
  • Avoid being listed on CRB: Pay all your other loans on time

New users start with Ksh 100 - Ksh 1,000. Active users can grow to Ksh 5,000, Ksh 20,000, up to Ksh 70,000.

Fuliza Charges 2026

Fuliza charges a one-time access fee + daily maintenance fee. You pay when you use it:

  • Ksh 1 - 100: Access fee Ksh 0, Daily fee Ksh 0
  • Ksh 101 - 500: Access fee Ksh 3, Daily fee Ksh 6
  • Ksh 501 - 1,000: Access fee Ksh 6, Daily fee Ksh 12
  • Ksh 1,001 - 1,500: Access fee Ksh 9, Daily fee Ksh 18
  • Ksh 1,501 - 2,500: Access fee Ksh 12, Daily fee Ksh 25
  • Ksh 2,501 - 70,000: Access fee Ksh 25, Daily fee Ksh 30

Tip: Repay the same day to avoid daily fees adding up.

How To Repay Fuliza

You don't need a special code. Just deposit money to your M-Pesa. Fuliza will deduct automatically - first the fees, then the amount you borrowed. To check if you have cleared, dial *234# > Fuliza > Check Balance. It should show Ksh 0.

How To Opt Out of Fuliza

Dial *234# > Fuliza M-Pesa > Opt Out. You must first repay all your Fuliza balance before you can opt out.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get Fuliza if I am on CRB?
No. You must clear CRB listing first and build good M-Pesa history.

Why is my Fuliza limit 0?
You are new, inactive, or you have an unpaid Fuliza balance. Use M-Pesa more and repay on time.

Can I borrow Fuliza and send to another number?
Yes. If you don't have enough balance, Fuliza will top up and the transaction will go through.

Final Advice

Fuliza is good for emergencies, not for daily borrowing. The daily fee can become expensive if you don't repay fast. Borrow only what you can repay today or tomorrow.
