Branch Loan App 2026 - How To Get 100k Fast

Branch is one of the best loan apps in Kenya. You can borrow from Ksh 250 to Ksh 100,000 without collateral, without paperwork, direct to M-PESA in minutes.

Quick Facts:
- First loan: Ksh 250 - Ksh 5,000
- Max loan: Up to Ksh 100,000
- Interest: About 10% - 18% per month
- Repayment: 4 weeks to 6 months
- Disbursement: In minutes to M-PESA

How To Apply For Branch Loan - Step By Step

Step 1: Download Branch App from Play Store

Step 2: Register with your Safaricom number and ID

Step 3: Allow Branch to check your M-PESA messages. This is how they decide your limit. Do not delete your messages.

Step 4: You will see your limit. Tap Apply.

Step 5: Money comes to M-PESA in 2-5 minutes.

Why Branch Rejects You & How To Fix It

1. Low M-PESA activity: Use M-PESA more. Send, receive, pay bills with M-PESA for 2-3 months.

2. You are on CRB: Clear your other small loans first, then wait 30 days.

3. New Safaricom line: Branch trusts old lines. Use a line that is 6+ months old.

4. You deleted messages: Branch needs your messages to see your income. Keep them.

How To Increase Branch Limit To 100k Fast

1. Borrow small and repay early: Take Ksh 1,000, repay in 3 days. Repeat 3 times.

2. Never delay: Even 1 day late drops your limit.

3. Use M-PESA daily: The more you transact, the higher your limit.

4. Repay in full: Do not pay half. Pay full amount.

Most people reach Ksh 20,000 in 3 months, and Ksh 50,000 - 100,000 in 8-12 months if they repay on time.

Is Branch Legit?

Yes. Branch is licensed by Central Bank of Kenya. It is safe. Never send money to anyone claiming to unlock your Branch limit. Branch never asks for money before giving a loan.
