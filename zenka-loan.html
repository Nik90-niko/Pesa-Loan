How to Get Zenka Loan

First Loan FREE • Up to KES 200,000 • 2026 Guide

Quick Facts

Limit: KES 500 - 200,000
Fee: 9% - 39% of loan (shown before you accept)
Repay: Up to 61 days
How: App or Dial *841#
Licensed: CBK Licensed - Zenka Digital Ltd

How To Apply - App

Step 1: Download Zenka Loan App from Play Store
Step 2: Register with your phone number + National ID
Step 3: Choose amount & repayment period
Step 4: Check total repayment - Accept if happy
Step 5: Done! Money sent to your M-Pesa ✅

How To Apply - No Smartphone? Use USSD

Step 1: Dial *841#
Step 2: Enter your PIN
Step 3: Select Apply for loan
Step 4: Choose amount & period

Note: USSD limit is lower, up to ~KES 10,000.

How To Increase Zenka Limit

✅ Repay ON TIME every time
✅ Repay early if you can
✅ Use M-Pesa regularly
✅ Don't borrow from many apps at once

Example

Borrow KES 1,000 for 61 days = Pay back KES 1,390. Your exact fee is shown in the app BEFORE you accept. Always check it!

Warning

Zenka is expensive if you are late. Late penalty is 1% per day! Repay on time to grow your limit and avoid CRB listing. First loan is often free - use it wisely.
