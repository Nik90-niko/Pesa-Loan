Eazzy Loan 2026 - How To Borrow From Equity Bank

KES 100 to KES 3,000,000 • Dial *247# • Instant to Equity Account

What is Eazzy Loan?

Eazzy Loan is Equity Bank's instant mobile loan. You borrow using your phone, no paperwork, no guarantor. Money goes to your Equity account and you can send it to M-Pesa.

Limit: KES 100 - 3,000,000
Repayment: 30 days
How to get: *247# or Equity Mobile App or Equitel

Check on Equity Bank Website →

How To Apply - Step by Step

1. Dial *247# on your Safaricom / Airtel line
2. Select Loans > Eazzy Loan
3. Select Apply Loan
4. Enter amount you want
5. Enter your Equity PIN to confirm
6. Money is sent to your Equity Account instantly!

Or use Equity Mobile App > Borrow > Eazzy Loan

Requirements

✅ Equity Bank Account (or Equitel line)
✅ Active for at least 3-6 months
✅ Good repayment history
✅ Must have an Equity PIN

How To Increase Your Eazzy Loan Limit

1. Repay on time, every time
2. Keep money moving in your Equity account - deposit often
3. Borrow small and repay fast 3-4 times
4. Use Equitel / Equity App regularly
5. Don't have other defaulted loans - clear CRB

How To Repay

Dial *247# > Loans > Repay Loan > Enter amount. Or use Equity App > Loans > Repay.

Warning: Late repayment = penalty + your limit drops + CRB listing. Always repay before day 30.

FAQs

Can I get Eazzy Loan without Equity account?
No. You must open Equity account or Equitel line first.

How long does approval take?
Instant, less than 1 minute if you qualify.

What if I get "You do not qualify"?
Use account for 3 months, save small amounts, then try again.
